Moldy Fruit

Pick two Illinois members of Congress. Each one is drawn as a fruit. The spots follow a mold level from 0 to 100.

Satire and opinion visualization. The mold level is an editorial score of public records. It is not a government rating, not a legal finding, and not an accusation of a crime. Party is shown so you can see who is who. Party is not an input to the score.

Mold level = other-committee share of receipts (up to 50) + 3.5 times the missed-vote percentage (up to 35) + public ethics points (0, 8, or 15). Halves round up. The methodology has the full rule, the retrieval date, and the limits. If a component has no source, the page says no data and does not guess.
