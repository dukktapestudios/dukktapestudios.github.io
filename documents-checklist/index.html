Documents Checklist

A generic list anyone can use. Check “I have that” for papers you already keep. The page never asks you to type a name, number, or secret, and it never uploads a document.

Marks stay on this device. Checkmarks and the blank, partial, or filed packet status are saved in this browser only. They are not an account, they are not sent to a server, and another device starts blank. Clearing site data deletes them. The export file is document marks only. This is an education checklist, not legal, tax, medical, or benefits advice.

  • Original The document the issuer gave you. Keep it somewhere durable, separate from everyday copies.
  • Certified copy A copy an official office issues or stamps as official. A plain photocopy is not the same thing.
  • Encrypted backup A scan inside encrypted storage, kept apart from the only paper copy.

Yearly adult responsibilities schedule

A checklist only. This page does not send reminders, notifications, or calendar items. A checked box means done for the period named on that group. When the period ends, the box starts empty again. Reset a group to clear it sooner. These marks stay in this browser and are not included in the export file.

Daily

Applies to today only. Tomorrow these start unchecked.

  • Take medications only as prescribed. Do not type medicine names here.

  • Spend about five minutes looking. Do not type amounts or account numbers here.

  • Do one real task from a list you keep elsewhere. Do not type the task here.

Monthly

Applies to this month only. Next month these start unchecked.

  • Pay what is due. Do not type confirmation numbers here.

  • Read the statements for anything unexpected. Do not type transactions here.

  • Glance at the plan you keep elsewhere. Do not type figures here.

Quarterly

Applies to this quarter only. Next quarter these start unchecked.

  • See whether contributions are what you intended. Do not type balances here.

  • See whether coverage still fits. Do not type policy numbers here.

Twice a year

Applies to this half of the year only. The next half starts unchecked.

  • Follow the device instructions. Sealed-life models differ and may not use a battery change.

  • Clear lint the way the maker describes. This is a checkbox, not a repair guide.

Annually

Applies to this year only. Next year these start unchecked.

  • File the return that is due. Keep the papers in the Tax packet, not in this box.

  • Confirm a will or beneficiary choices still match the intent. Do not type names here.

  • Base this on clinician advice. This list is not medical advice and does not book a visit.

  • Change a critical login if it is weak, reused, or exposed. A strong unique login does not need a yearly swap. Do not type secrets here.

  • Walk the packets on this page. Do not type document details here.

Living onboarding is the packet mark for a category: blank, partial, or filed. Blank means not started. Partial means some papers are gathered. Filed means the papers you have are stored the way the guidance describes. Checking a box does not change that mark.

01 VA

Veterans benefits and service papers.

  • Keep the original in a fire-resistant folder, ask for a certified copy when an office requires one, and store an encrypted scan apart from the paper.

  • Keep the original letter, carry a plain copy to appointments, and put an encrypted backup with your other scans.

  • Keep the original at home, carry a copy, and use an encrypted backup for the scan.

  • Keep the official letter or PDF as the record, share a copy with a school, and store an encrypted backup.

  • Keep the original decision, use a copy for follow-up, and add an encrypted backup.

  • Keep these with private medical papers and use an encrypted backup, not an open shared folder.

02 Housing

Home ownership, rental, and insurance papers.

  • Keep the recorded document, order a certified copy when an office asks, and store an encrypted backup of the scan.

  • Keep the fully signed original or official PDF, leave a plain copy where you can reach it, and store an encrypted backup.

  • Keep the latest statement and any payoff letter as copies, with an encrypted backup. Do not type loan numbers here.

  • The declarations page is the useful copy. Keep it with the policy and store an encrypted backup.

  • Keep the latest bill, store older bills you still need as copies, and use an encrypted backup.

  • Keep the current rules and statements as copies and store an encrypted backup.

03 Tax

Returns and the papers that support them.

  • Keep the filed copy for the years the tax agency expects, and store an encrypted backup apart from the paper.

  • Keep the original forms the payer sent and store an encrypted backup. Do not type the amounts into this page.

  • A transcript from the tax agency is an official copy. Keep the PDF and an encrypted backup.

  • Keep the receipt copy with the return it belongs to and store an encrypted backup.

  • Keep the copies that support the return and store an encrypted backup. Do not list the details here.

  • Keep that letter offline with the tax papers. Do not type any code from it into this page.

04 Banking

Institution papers, without balances or numbers.

  • Keep the paper the institution gave you offline. Do not type account numbers into this page.

  • Store the original offline. This page is only a checkmark, not a place for routing numbers.

  • Keep the copy for as long as you need it and store an encrypted backup. Do not type balances here.

  • Keep the confirmation copy and an encrypted backup. Do not type any code from it into this page.

  • Keep the institution's copy and an encrypted backup. Do not type names or numbers here.

  • Keep the agreement copy. Do not make a list of the box contents on this page.

05 School

Enrollment, completion, and school health papers.

  • Keep a copy for your records and an encrypted backup of the PDF.

  • An official transcript is the certified copy a school issues. A student printout is only an everyday copy.

  • Store the original safely, order a certified copy when an office requires one, and keep an encrypted backup of a scan.

  • Keep the letter as a copy and an encrypted backup. Do not type award numbers here.

  • Give the school the copy it asked for and keep the original with medical papers, plus an encrypted backup.

06 Vehicles

Title, registration, and insurance papers.

  • Keep the original somewhere safer than the glove box, ask the motor agency about a certified copy, and store an encrypted scan apart from the paper.

  • The card you carry is an everyday copy. Keep the receipt and an encrypted backup.

  • Carry the card copy, keep the declarations page, and store an encrypted backup.

  • Keep the letter as a copy and an encrypted backup. Do not type loan numbers here.

  • Keep the current paper and drop older ones when the local rule says you may.

07 Identity

Civil records and identity credentials.

  • Keep the certified copy from vital records. A photocopy is not a certified copy. Store an encrypted scan apart from the paper.

  • The booklet is the original credential. Do not pack every other original in the same bag, and avoid an unencrypted photo of the page.

  • Keep the card offline. Never type the number into this page, a note, or the export.

  • The card is the credential. A photocopy is not a substitute. Keep any scan in encrypted storage, not in an open inbox.

  • Keep the original, use a certified copy only when an office accepts one, and store an encrypted backup.

  • Order the certified copy from the court or vital records office, use a photocopy day to day, and keep an encrypted backup.

08 Medical

Coverage and care papers. Names of medicines stay off this page.

  • Carry a copy if you need it and keep an encrypted backup. Do not type member numbers here.

  • Keep the signed original, give copies to the people who must have them, and store an encrypted backup.

  • Keep the list on paper or in encrypted storage. Do not type medicine names into this page.

  • Keep the official copy and an encrypted backup.

  • Keep the copies for the year you still need and store an encrypted backup. Do not type claim numbers here.

09 Benefits

Award letters and plan statements.

  • Keep the original letter, take a copy to appointments, and store an encrypted backup.

  • Keep the latest statement as a copy and an encrypted backup. Do not type plan numbers here.

  • Keep the current notice and an encrypted backup. Do not type case numbers here.

  • The booklet and any certificate are the copies to keep, with an encrypted backup.

  • Keep the decision copy and an encrypted backup.

10 Crypto / security notes

Habits and locations only. No secrets are entered here.

  • The manager holds secrets. This page only records that you use one.

  • Record the checkmark only. Do not enter codes or backup codes here.

  • Write the location on paper kept offline. Do not enter recovery words or codes on this page.

  • Keep the device offline. This page does not ask what is stored on it.

  • The backup should be encrypted and kept apart from the only copy of those files.

  • The note can point to a drawer in plain language. Do not paste instructions or any secret into this page.

Where to ask for an official copy

The agency that issued a document is the place to order a certified copy. This page does not order one and does not collect a form.

  1. Military service records
  2. Tax transcript
  3. Social Security number card
  4. Vital records, from USA.gov
  5. Everyday device safety, from CISA
