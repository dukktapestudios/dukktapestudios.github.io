1. How to learn how to learn

You don't need to know everything first. Use the curiosity loop:

Ask→Try→Check→Adjust→repeat
  • Ask: "Explain ___ like I'm new to it."
  • Try: do one small thing right away.
  • Check: did it work? What surprised you?
  • Adjust: ask a follow-up and try again.

Mistakes are data. Each loop makes you a little better.

2. Attacking a problem

  1. Say it in one sentence. "I want ___ so that ___."
  2. Break it into small steps. Each step should take 15 minutes or less.
  3. Ask AI for options. "Give me 3 ways to do this, with pros and cons."
  4. Pick one and start. Start doing. You can change course later.

Stuck? Ask: "What is the smallest next step I can do right now?"

3. Idea to prototype

Idea→Prompt→Test→Modify
Worked example: a feed-time reminder poster

Idea: A poster for the barn showing feeding times.

Prompt: "Make a simple printable schedule for feeding 3 horses at 6 AM and 5 PM. Big text, easy to read."

Test: Print it. Is it readable from across the barn? No, the text is too small.

Modify: "Make the times twice as big and add a checkbox for each feeding."

Two or three loops later, you have something real.

4. Prompting basics

  • Role: who the AI should act as.
  • Goal: what you want.
  • Context: details that matter.
  • Format: how the answer should look.
  • Examples: show what good looks like.
  • Iterate: the first answer is a draft. Ask for changes.

5. Creating in every medium


✍️ Writing
Good for: drafts, emails, stories, outlines, editing.
Starter prompt: "Write a short, friendly post about ___. Then give me 3 other versions."
Tools: chat assistants such as ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok.


🖼️ Images
Good for: logos, concept art, thumbnails, mockups.
Starter prompt: "A ___ in the style of ___, bright colors, plain background."
Tools: image generators such as Midjourney, DALL·E (in ChatGPT), Adobe Firefly.


🎬 Video
Good for: short clips, b-roll, ideas for scenes.
Starter prompt: "5-second shot of ___, slow camera move, sunset light."
Tools: video generators such as Runway, Sora, Google Veo.


🎵 Music and voice
Good for: background music, jingles, voiceovers.
Starter prompt: "Upbeat country instrumental, 30 seconds, for a ranch video intro."
Tools: Suno, Udio (music); ElevenLabs (voice).


💻 Code and websites
Good for: small web pages, tools, scripts, fixing errors.
Starter prompt: "Make a one-page website for ___ with a title, 3 sections, and a contact link. Explain each part simply."
Tools: chat assistants, Cursor, GitHub Copilot; free hosting like GitHub Pages.


🧊 3D and printing
Good for: ideas, simple parts, fixing measurements.
Starter prompt: "Help me design a simple ___ holder. List the measurements I need to take."
Tools: Tinkercad and Fusion 360 for modeling; slicers like Cura, PrusaSlicer, Chitubox for printing.


Tools change fast. Learn the loop, and it will work with any tool.

7. Safety and honesty


  • AI makes mistakes. It can sound sure and still be wrong.
  • Check facts on important things (health, money, law, animals) with a trusted source or expert.
  • Protect your info. Don't paste passwords, card numbers, or private details.
  • Be honest. Say when something was made with AI, and respect other people's work.
