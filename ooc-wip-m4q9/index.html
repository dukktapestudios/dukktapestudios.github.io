Quick-start rules

A Dukktape Studios parody card game. It's all the same game. We just call stuff different things.

Goal

Get your opponent's Sanity from 20 to 0. (Or they run out of cards in their Stack o' Stuff and must draw: they lose.)

Setup

  • Each player brings a Stack o' Stuff of 40 cards (about 16 Juice Sources + 24 other cards, 1–2 colors).
  • Start at 20 Sanity. Shuffle, draw 7. Don't like your hand? Shuffle back and draw 6.

Your turn

  1. Untappy-Tap all your stuff.
  2. Draw 1 card (first player skips this on turn 1).
  3. Main Phase: Play 1 Juice Source per turn. Tappy-Tap Juice Sources for Juice to play cards.
  4. Start Drama: Tappy-Tap Things to attack. The opponent may Nope (block) with untapped Things. Things deal damage equal to power; damage ≥ toughness = it goes to The Shame Pile. Un-Noped damage hits Sanity.
  5. Second Main Phase, then end turn (max 7 cards in hand — Yeet extras).

Cost reading

"2 + 1 Gas Station" = 2 Juice of any color + 1 Gas Station Juice. New Things can't Start Drama the turn they arrive (unless Zoom Zoom).

Glossary

Tappy-Tap = tap · Untappy-Tap = untap · Juice = resource · Stack o' Stuff = deck · The Shame Pile = discard pile · Yeet = discard · Throw Under the Bus = sacrifice · Sanity = life · Start Drama = attack · Nope = block · Equip = attach to your Thing

Keywords

  • Zoomies — Can only be Noped by Things with Zoomies.
  • Bulldoze — Extra damage beyond the Noper's toughness hits Sanity.
  • Zoom Zoom — Can Start Drama the turn it arrives.
  • Self Care — Damage it deals also gains you that much Sanity.
  • Bad Vibes — Any damage it deals to a Thing destroys it.
  • Couch Potato — Can't Start Drama.

Color guide

  • Beige (Suburban). Order, rules, Sanity gain, shutting things down. HOAs, coupons, managers.

  • Blue-Light (Online). Draw cards, counter stuff, tricks. Doomscrolling, tech support, reply guys.

  • Gas Station (Chaos). Fast, aggressive, direct damage. Energy drinks, check engine lights.

  • Swamp Ass (Shady). Destruction, Sanity drain, bringing stuff back. Tax evasion, your ex.

  • Lawn (Outdoorsy). Big Things, growth, extra Juice. Raccoons, geese, a 1993 Civic.

  • Colorless. Definitely Not Artifacts. Anyone can play them.
