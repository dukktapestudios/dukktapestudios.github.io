GI Bill: from apply to sitting in class

One step at a time. Do that step, then stop. This page is for days when focus is short.

Not official VA guidance. Not legal or financial advice. Verify your benefit, your months, and your school on VA.gov (opens in a new tab) and with your School Certifying Official.

The path

Eight steps. Finish one. Then stop. Come back for the next one.

Next: Know your benefit level and months left.

Jump to the next step

  1. 1Sign in and confirm you already applied

    Sign in on VA.gov education benefits (opens in a new tab). If you already have a Statement of Benefits or a COE, download that proof and move on.

    If you have never applied, use Form 22-1990 online. You will need your Social Security number, direct deposit, and a school if you already know it. VA.gov has listed an average decision time of about 30 days. Your letter may take a different amount of time.

    Then stop.

  2. 2Get proof for the school

    Download or print the Statement of Benefits or the COE. Keep the PDF on your phone. Give it to the School Certifying Official (SCO). Ask for the SCO by that title.

    Read what a COE is (opens in a new tab) if the words are fuzzy.

    Then stop.

  3. 3Pick one VA-approved program

    Open the GI Bill Comparison Tool (opens in a new tab). Confirm the school is approved. Pick one program: a degree, a trade, or a certificate.

    Then stop.

  4. 4Apply and get accepted

    Apply through the school admissions office. When you apply, ask for the veterans office or the SCO contact.

    Then stop.

  5. 5Register for the term

    Register for courses the approved program requires. Save the schedule. Tell the SCO you are using Chapter 33.

    Then stop.

  6. 6Give the SCO your proof

    Email or upload the Statement of Benefits or COE, plus your schedule. Ask the SCO to certify you in Enrollment Manager.

    VA guidance used by schools says Chapter 33 can be certified up to 180 days before the term starts, and should be certified within 30 days after it starts. Ask your SCO which date they use.

    Then stop.

  7. 7Watch the payment setup

    Tuition and fees go to the school. Housing and book money, when you are more than half time, go to you. Confirm direct deposit on VA.gov.

    Talk to the SCO before you drop a class. A drop can create a debt.

    Then stop.

  8. 8Verify enrollment on day one

    Show up. Post-9/11 students verify enrollment each month, online or by text. Housing payments need that verification and an active enrollment.

    The steps are on VA.gov: verify school enrollment (opens in a new tab).

    Then stop. You are in class.

If you get stuck

Education Call Center

1-888-GIBILL-1 (1-888-442-4551)

Monday through Friday, about 7 a.m. to 6 p.m. Central.

  • One step per day. Write only that step title on a sticky note.
  • Save every PDF in one folder named School.
  • If focus or a low mood stalls you, text a friend only the next step title and a day. Example: "Step 3, by Friday." If you need to talk with someone now, call or text 988.
  • The day you are accepted, put the next term open date on your calendar.
  • You can also ask a question at Ask VA (opens in a new tab).
