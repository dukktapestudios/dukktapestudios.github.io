Same car. More buyers. One dealer quote.

Group Lot gathers people who want the same vehicle, then asks a dealer for a volume price. You still buy your own car from the dealer — you just don’t negotiate alone.

Not a shared bank account. Nobody dumps the full car price into a pot here. That gets legally messy. This demo only tracks interest. When a lot fills, a dealer quotes the group; each person signs their own deal.

Straight answers

Why not just pool the money and buy in bulk?

Holding everyone’s car money looks like escrow or acting like a dealer, which can need licenses. Safer model: pool the demand, get one volume quote, each person buys from the dealer.

Do I still go to a dealership?

Usually yes for paperwork and pickup — but the price fight is already done as a group. Some dealers will deliver.

Is this a live service?

No. This is a Dukktape Studios concept demo. Joins stay on your device (localStorage). Not legal advice.
