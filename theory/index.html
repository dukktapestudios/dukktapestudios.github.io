How I see life and reality

This is a living document, a work in progress I'll keep refining.

  2. Every outcome

Set in motion

God set the universe in motion and walked away. That's the only way free will exists.

He guides lightly, through patterns and recognitions.

"In his image" means consciousness. The same consciousness at different levels. God is the highest.

Every outcome

I see the multiverse as bubbles for every possible action. Every outcome exists.

God knows all outcomes. He wants you to choose the good one.

Continuity

The Bible's divinity is its continuity through translations.

Christianity is the only religion you can make fun of without backlash.

A society with about 51% living by it functions, but not well — the more who follow it, the better it runs.

A sine wave

I see the Trinity as a sine wave. The Father is the positive peak. The Son is the zero crossing. The Holy Spirit is the negative peak.

Energy becomes matter. Matter becomes energy.

An antenna

The brain is an antenna. God is consciousness, like a carrier signal. Differently shaped brains pick up different wavelengths. That's why we're different while sharing the divine.

A bucket

There's a bucket of consciousness. At birth you dip a cup in. Life fills it. At death it pours back, mixes, and the next person draws from it.

That's why talents recur, and why people feel they've lived before.
