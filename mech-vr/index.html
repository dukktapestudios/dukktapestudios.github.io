Click the view, then use the mouse to look. W A S D walks with momentum. Q and E snap the hull. Left click fires the cannon. Hold F or the right mouse button to swing the arc blade. On a headset, Enter VR: the left stick walks, the right stick snap-turns, the right trigger fires, and the left grip swings.
