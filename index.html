About

Hi, I'm Dukktape. I teach myself how the internet and AI work by turning ideas into real things: websites, programs, and games. I use AI to patchwork them together. They're usually held together with duct tape, but working is working.

My goal is to show you that you can just start. It doesn't have to be pretty or perfect. Build it rough, hobble it together, and figure it out as you go.
