Make and sell your first simple book on Amazon KDP

The short version

KDP prints your paperback only when someone orders it, so you pay nothing upfront. Amazon keeps the printing cost out of each sale and pays you a royalty. Your job is to make two PDF files (the inside pages and the cover), fill in the book details honestly, order a proof copy to check it, and then publish.

01What KDP is

Checked Nov 22, 2026

Kindle Direct Publishing (KDP) is Amazon's free self-publishing service for eBooks, paperbacks and hardcovers. On its home page, Amazon says KDP gives you free tools to self-publish in more than 10 countries and over 45 languages, and that books can appear on Amazon stores in about 72 hours (KDP home).

Paperbacks are printed on demand. KDP's printing cost page says that means "you don't have to pay any costs upfront or carry any inventory." The printing cost is taken out of your royalty instead (Paperback Printing Cost).

You control

  • The inside pages and the cover
  • The trim size, ink and paper
  • The list price (within KDP's minimum and maximum)
  • Title, description, keywords and categories

Amazon handles

  • Printing each copy when it sells
  • Shipping to the customer
  • Customer payment and the product page
  • Paying your royalties each month

02Pick your book type

Checked Nov 22, 2026 · Source: Low-Content Books, Guide to Kindle Content Quality

Beginners often start with a "simple" book. KDP sorts simple books in a way that surprises people: a puzzle book is not a low-content book.

  • Low-content books are, in KDP's words, books with "minimal or no content on the interior pages," usually repetitive and "designed to be filled in by the user." KDP lists notebooks, planners, journals, log books and score card templates as examples.
  • Activity books like puzzle books and coloring books are listed by KDP as "Not Generally Low-Content," because their pages are not the same over and over.
  • Written books (novels, nonfiction, children's stories, manuals) are regular books too.

Remember

If you publish a true low-content book, you must tick the Low-content box under Categories. KDP warns that "if you do not select this box, or select a different box, your book will be rejected." Don't tick it for a puzzle or coloring book.

03Free tools and AI help (and the rules)

Checked Nov 22, 2026 · Source: KDP Tools and Resources, Content Guidelines

Free tools

From Amazon

Manuscript templates and the cover calculator

KDP offers free Word manuscript templates in each trim size, and a cover calculator and template generator that gives exact cover sizes as a PDF or PNG guide.

From Amazon

Kindle Create and Cover Creator

Kindle Create is best for text-heavy books. KDP notes it "isn't supported for paperbacks with tables, footnotes, or images that bleed" (Format Your Paperback), so puzzle books usually need another tool. Cover Creator makes simple covers inside KDP.

Free desktop apps

LibreOffice, Scribus, Inkscape, GIMP

LibreOffice (word processor that exports PDF), Scribus (page layout), Inkscape (vector drawing, good for mazes and grids) and GIMP (photo editing) are free and open source. Any tool works as long as the final files meet KDP's specs.

Using AI: what KDP requires

AI can help you brainstorm, write puzzle generators, draft text or make images. KDP's Content Guidelines split AI use into two kinds:

You must tell KDP

AI-generated

"If you used an AI-based tool to create the actual content (whether text, images, or translations), it is considered 'AI-generated,' even if you applied substantial edits afterwards." This includes "cover and interior images and artwork."

No need to tell KDP

AI-assisted

"If you created the content yourself, and used AI-based tools to edit, refine, error-check, or otherwise improve that content," it is AI-assisted. Using AI "to brainstorm and generate ideas" while you make the text or images yourself also counts as AI-assisted.

KDP says you must tell it about AI-generated content "when you publish a new book or make edits to and republish an existing book." Either way, "You are responsible for verifying that all AI-generated and/or AI-assisted content adheres to all content guidelines, including by complying with all applicable intellectual property rights."

Quality rules apply to every book, AI-made or not. KDP's Guide to Kindle Content Quality lists "disappointing content" it doesn't allow, including content "freely available on the web" (unless you own it), content "not significantly different from content in another book," content that is "too short," and content "excessively reused, recycled, or repeated within or across books." The Content Guidelines also say KDP removes books whose descriptions "mislead customers."

Remember

When in doubt, answer the AI question honestly. If an AI wrote the puzzles or drew the pictures, that is AI-generated, even if you fixed them afterwards. Solve every puzzle yourself; a broken puzzle is a poor customer experience no matter who made it.

Check it works

You can say in one sentence which parts of your book are AI-generated, which are AI-assisted and which you made yourself.

04Trim size, bleed and margins

Checked Nov 22, 2026 · Source: Set Trim Size, Bleed, and Margins, Print Options

Trim size

Trim size is the finished width and height of the book. KDP says the most common US size is 6" x 9" (a regular trim size). Any book "more than 6.12 inches (155.5 mm) in width or more than 9 inches (228.6 mm) in height" is a large trim size, which costs more to print. Custom paperback sizes must be 4"–8.5" wide and 6"–11.69" tall.

Bleed

"Bleed" means printing right to the edge of the page. KDP trims every page by 0.125" (3.2 mm) at the top, bottom and outside edge. If anything on your inside pages touches the edge, set the whole file up with bleed: page width = trim width + 0.125", page height = trim height + 0.25". KDP notes that "Even if just one page in your interior requires bleed, then your entire file should be set up with bleed." All covers need bleed.

Margins

Outside margins (top, bottom and outer edge) must be at least 0.25" without bleed, or 0.375" with bleed. The inside margin (the "gutter," next to the spine) grows with page count:

Remember

These are minimums. Leaving a little more space (for example 0.5" all round) makes puzzle pages easier to write on and safer to print.

05Make the printable interior PDF

Checked Nov 22, 2026 · Source: Paperback Submission Guidelines, Format Your Paperback

5.1Set the page size in your tool to your trim size (or trim size plus bleed), then set margins from the table above before adding anything.

Check it works

A test page exported to PDF shows the exact size you expect in your PDF viewer's document properties.

5.2Build the pages: a title page, a copyright page, the puzzles or content, answer pages, and page numbers. KDP asks for page numbers in order, "with even numbers on left pages and odd numbers on the right" for left-to-right books.

Remember

KDP lists "excessive blank pages," missing pages and wrong page numbers as common reasons files fail.

5.3Use readable text and sharp images. KDP's minimum font size is 7 points, and images should be at least 300 DPI. KDP recommends staying at or below 600 DPI and keeping files under 650 MB.

Check it works

Zoom the PDF to 200%. Lines and grids stay crisp, not blurry or blocky.

5.4Export a PDF with all fonts embedded and layers flattened. Leave out crop marks, trim marks, comments, bookmarks, placeholder text and watermarks, which KDP lists as failure points.

Check it works

Your PDF viewer's font list shows every font as "Embedded," and no "lorem ipsum" or "TODO" text is left anywhere.

06Make the cover

Checked Nov 22, 2026 · Source: Create a Paperback Cover, KDP Cover Calculator, Barcodes

A paperback cover is one PDF with the back cover, spine and front cover side by side as a single image. The easiest path:

  1. Finish the interior first, so you know the exact page count.
  2. Open the KDP cover calculator, enter your ink and paper, trim size and page count, and download the template (PDF and PNG).
  3. Put the template on a bottom layer in your design app, design over it, hide the template, and export a PDF.

The numbers behind the template

Spine width (black ink, white paper) = page count × 0.002252"
Spine width (black ink, cream paper) = page count × 0.0025"
Cover width  = bleed + back width + spine + front width + bleed
Cover height = bleed + trim height + bleed      (bleed = 0.125")
  • Spine text needs roughly 80 pages or more. KDP's cover page says "at least 79 pages," its submission guidelines say "more than 79 pages," and Cover Creator needs 80. Thinner books need a blank spine.
  • Keep important text at least 0.25" in from the outside cover edges.
  • Leave the barcode box clear: KDP places a barcode in a 2" x 1.2" white box in the lower right of the back cover if you don't supply one, and anything there gets covered.
  • Covers print on 80 lb (220 GSM) stock, glossy or matte (Print Options).

Check it works

Your cover PDF's width and height match the calculator's "full cover" size exactly, and the title is readable as a small thumbnail.

07ISBN: the free KDP one and its limits

Checked Nov 22, 2026 · Source: What is an ISBN and Imprint?, Low-Content Books

An ISBN is a book's unique ID number. Each format (paperback, hardcover) needs its own. KDP offers a free ISBN for paperbacks and hardcovers, or you can buy your own.

  • Free KDP ISBNs can only be used on KDP. In KDP's words, "They cannot be used to publish outside of KDP."
  • With a free KDP ISBN, the publisher (imprint) shows as "Independently published."
  • Low-content books are "not eligible for the free KDP ISBN." They can use your own ISBN or publish without one.
  • eBooks don't need an ISBN.
  • You can't change your ISBN choice after publishing without unpublishing and republishing.

Remember

For a first puzzle or activity book sold only on Amazon, the free KDP ISBN is the simple choice. Buy your own only if you plan to sell the same edition through other printers or stores.

08Printing cost, list price and royalties

Checked Nov 22, 2026 · Source: Paperback Royalty, Paperback Printing Cost

The two formulas

Printing cost = fixed cost + (page count × per-page cost)
Royalty       = (royalty rate × list price) − printing cost

Printing cost depends on page count, ink, paper, regular or large trim, and which Amazon store the order comes from. KDP says "Bleed settings and cover finish don't affect printing cost."

Color costs much more. For example, premium color with 42–828 pages on Amazon.com is $1.00 + $0.065 per page (regular trim). That is one reason most puzzle and coloring books print in black ink.

Minimum and maximum list price

KDP won't let you price below a minimum, worked out as printing cost ÷ royalty rate (0.50 or 0.60, depending on which price band you are in). The maximum paperback list price is $250 on Amazon.com and £250 on Amazon.co.uk. Use KDP's Printing Cost & Royalty Calculator to see exact numbers for every store.

Remember

On Amazon.com, one cent can change your royalty a lot: $9.98 earns 50%, $9.99 earns 60%. Customers may see a discounted price, but KDP says that "won't affect your royalties."

09Worked example: a 110-page puzzle book

Checked Nov 22, 2026 · Rates from Paperback Printing Cost, Paperback Royalty, Create a Paperback Cover

Example calculation only

This is a made-up book used to show the math with KDP's published formulas. It is not a sales forecast, and KDP's own calculator is the final word. Results are rounded to the nearest cent here; KDP's exact rounding wasn't checked.

The book: 110 pages, black ink, white paper, 8.5" x 11" (a large trim size), sold on Amazon.com.

1. Printing cost

110 pages falls in the 24–110 page band (fixed cost only)
Large trim, Amazon.com fixed cost = $2.84
Printing cost = $2.84

Note: KDP's table lists 110 pages in both bands, but its note says "Black-ink paperbacks with 24 - 110 pages only incur the fixed cost." The other band would give $1.00 + 110 × $0.017 = $2.87, so the difference is tiny either way.

2. Royalty at two prices

At $9.99 (60% band):  0.60 × $9.99 = $5.994
                      $5.994 − $2.84 = $3.154  ≈ $3.15 per sale

At $8.99 (50% band):  0.50 × $8.99 = $4.495
                      $4.495 − $2.84 = $1.655  ≈ $1.66 per sale

Pricing $1 higher nearly doubles the royalty here, because $9.99 crosses into the 60% band.

3. Minimum list price

50% band: $2.84 ÷ 0.50 = $5.68 minimum list price

4. Expanded Distribution (optional, 40%)

At $9.99: 0.40 × $9.99 = $3.996 − $2.84 = $1.156  ≈ $1.16 per sale

5. Same idea on Amazon.co.uk (A4, £8.99)

A4 (8.27" x 11.69") is a large trim; UK fixed cost = £2.15
£8.99 is at or above £7.99, so the rate is 60%
0.60 × £8.99 = £5.394 − £2.15 = £3.244  ≈ £3.24 per sale

KDP notes that in some stores Amazon adds local VAT or GST on top of the list price you enter, so the price shoppers see can differ.

6. Cover size for the US book

Spine  = 110 × 0.002252" = 0.2477"   (110 is over 79, so spine text is allowed)
Width  = 0.125 + 8.5 + 0.2477 + 8.5 + 0.125 = 17.4977"
Height = 0.125 + 11 + 0.125 = 11.25"
Interior page size with bleed = 8.625" x 11.25"
Inside margin (24–150 pages) = at least 0.375"

10eBook royalties: 35% and 70%

Checked Nov 22, 2026 · Source: eBook Royalties, eBook List Price Requirements, Digital Book Pricing Page

Puzzle, coloring and fill-in books don't suit Kindle (see book types), but a written companion or guide could also be an eBook. Each eBook uses one of two royalty options:

eBook royalty options on Amazon.com

Swipe or scroll sideways to see every column.

OptionFormulaPrice range (Amazon.com)Catches
35%0.35 × (list price − VAT)From $0.99 (under 3 MB), $1.99 (3–10 MB) or $2.99 (10 MB+), up to $200Works in every territory
70%0.70 × (list price − VAT − delivery cost)$2.99–$12.99 (the top was $9.99 until July 7, 2026)Delivery cost is $0.15 per MB on Amazon.com. Only for buyers in KDP's 70% territories; others pay 35%. The eBook must be at least 20% below the paperback's list price.

Example calculation (made-up numbers): a 2 MB eBook at $4.99, sold to a US buyer with no VAT, on the 70% option: delivery cost = 2 × $0.15 = $0.30, so royalty = 0.70 × ($4.99 − $0.30) = $3.283 ≈ $3.28.

KDP's pricing page also says books that are mainly public domain content only get 35%.

11Keywords and categories

Checked Nov 22, 2026 · Source: Make Your Book More Discoverable with Keywords, KDP Categories, Metadata Guidelines

Up to 7

Keywords

  • KDP allows "up to seven keywords or short phrases."
  • Think like a shopper: "horse puzzle book for kids," "word search ages 8-12."
  • Search Amazon with each one first and look at the suggestions.
  • Avoid words already in your title, quality claims like "best," time words like "new," other authors' names, and brands you don't own.

Up to 3

Categories

  • KDP lets you pick 3 categories, like shelves in a store.
  • Choose accurate ones. KDP says it may move books placed in unrelated categories.
  • Category changes can take up to 72 hours to show.

Remember

KDP says it has "a zero tolerance policy for metadata that is meant to advertise, promote, or mislead." Honest keywords are also the ones that bring buyers who stay happy.

12Proof copies and author copies

Checked Nov 22, 2026 · Source: How do I order a proof or author copy?, How much do proof or author copies cost?

  • Proof copies are for checking a book before it goes live. You can order them while the book is a Draft, after approving it in the Print Previewer, up to 5 at a time.
  • Author copies are for after launch (status "Live"), up to 999 per order.
  • Both cost the book's printing cost per copy, plus shipping and tax. KDP says they don't get free or Prime shipping.

Check it works

Hold the proof in your hands. Nothing important is cut off at the edges or lost in the spine, every puzzle can be solved with a pencil, every answer is right, and the spine text is centered.

13Taxes basics (not tax advice)

Checked Nov 22, 2026 · Source: Complete Tax Profile, Tax Information, Amazon Tax Information Interview Guide

Not tax advice

This section only explains what KDP asks for. For your own situation, talk to a qualified tax professional or check the IRS (or your country's tax office).

  • KDP requires a tax profile. It says "We must receive and validate your tax identity before you can update existing books or publish new books."
  • The tax interview is designed to collect the information for an IRS W-9 (for U.S. persons) or W-8 (for non-U.S. persons). Amazon's guide says the W-9 "should be completed by U.S. persons only."
  • For U.S. persons, Amazon's guide describes reporting on Form 1099-MISC, which you can get electronically if you consent.
  • For non-U.S. publishers, KDP says the default U.S. withholding rate is 30%, possibly lower under a tax treaty.
  • On whether royalties are taxable, KDP's Tax Information page answers: "The short answer: yes."

More on the form itself: IRS: About Form W-9.

14Upload limits and when you get paid

Checked Nov 22, 2026 · Source: Create a Book, When will I get paid?

  • New title limits: KDP lets you create "Up to 2 titles per book format each week" and submit up to 2 new titles per format each week. That alone rules out flooding the store with dozens of quick books.
  • Payments: KDP says "Royalties are paid every month, approximately 60 days after the end of the month in which the sale was reported" (90 days for Expanded Distribution), once you reach the payment minimum.

15Example idea: a horse-themed activity book

Checked Nov 22, 2026 · Rules from Content Guidelines, Guide to Kindle Content Quality

Here is a generic first-book idea to show how the pieces fit together.

Option A

"Horse Puzzle Adventures" activity book

8.5" x 11", black ink, about 100–110 pages: horse-breed word searches, stable mazes, "spot the difference" pony pictures, tack-matching puzzles, simple horse-care quizzes and a full answer key. Paperback only, since KDP says puzzle books aren't suited to Kindle.

Option B

A companion book for a card game called Hoofprint Legends

A rule-explainer, strategy tips, printable score sheets, deck-building puzzles and coloring pages of the game's horses. This only works if you made the game or have written permission. Content and trademark rights belong to the game's owner, and KDP's quality guide says companion guides based on other books "are not allowed, with limited exceptions."

Remember

Score sheets alone would be a low-content book (tick the Low-content box). Add real puzzles and explanations and it becomes a regular book that can use the free KDP ISBN.

16Inspiration: a 110-page maths game book

Checked Nov 22, 2026 · Source: post on X by @learnwith_games, publisher's book page

On November 22, 2026, the educational site Learn With Games (@learnwith_games) posted on X that an AI assistant "helped turn our free maths games into a real, physical book." The post says the assistant (Grok Bot) "wrote and tested 40 pencil-and-paper games, laid out the pages, made the cover and set it up on Amazon KDP."

The publisher's own book page describes Jigby's Year 3 Maths Game Book as "A 110-page paperback, £8.99," with 40 games and 94 rounds for ages 7–8, answers for every round, and pages "Printed in black and white on large A4 pages."

Why it's useful for beginners: it matches the worked example above almost exactly (110 pages, black ink, A4 large trim, £8.99 on Amazon.co.uk), and it builds on games the creators already owned. Under KDP's rules, content an AI tool created counts as AI-generated and must be disclosed.

These details are the publisher's own claims. The Amazon listing, sales and any AI disclosure were not checked independently.
