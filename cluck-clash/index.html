CLUCK CLASH

Three roosters. A handful of Grain. Last flock crowing wins.

How to play

The point

Giant monsters are at the farm town, and the roosters have to settle who leads the defense. Each player fields 3 roosters and a 30-card support deck. Knock out all 3 enemy roosters. You also win if the other player cannot draw a card at the start of their turn.

Your turn

  1. Draw 1 card. The first player skips this on turn 1.
  2. Feed. Attach 1 Grain from your hand to any of your roosters. If you have Grain, you attach it.
  3. Support. Play any number of Actions and Gear, and 1 Coop. Counters are for the other player's turn.
  4. Switch once, if you want. Discard Grain from the active rooster equal to its switch cost, then swap with a bench rooster.
  5. Attack once. You need at least that much Grain on the rooster. Grain stays attached. The first player cannot attack on turn 1. Your turn ends after the attack.

Dice

  • Roll a d6 for each attack die. Meet the Hit number to deal that attack's full damage. A miss does nothing.
  • A natural 6 always hits and adds +20 once. A natural 1 always misses.
  • Extra dice each deal the full damage again. Only one critical +20 is added.
  • +1 to hit lowers the number you need. −1 raises it. The target number never goes below 2+ or above 6+.
  • A die can be rerolled only once. You keep the second roll.
  • Weakness is +20 once when an attack from that flock hits. It is not added per die.

Other rules on this table

  • Damage stays on a rooster when it goes to the bench. At 0 HP it is knocked out, and its owner picks a bench rooster to send in.
  • Gear: 1 per rooster, discarded when that rooster is knocked out. A new Coop replaces the old one.
  • Flock cards need one of your living roosters of that flock. Buffs and Grain from those cards go on that flock.
  • Golden Grain counts as 2 only on your main flock (the flock you brought the most of). On anyone else it is not played.
  • Signature moves are once per game, on your turn, before the attack. Roll 3+. A miss still uses the move up. Only one Signature a turn.
  • Traits are always on. Counters are played when their trigger happens.

Dawn Patrol is the fast flock. Night Forest is the slow one. The other lineups are one flock each.
