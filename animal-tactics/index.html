Legend
🪨 Rock / 🪵 Log / 🌳 Bush: cover. Shooting a unit standing on it needs +1 to hit. Rocks and logs block movement; bushes don't.
🍄 Mushroom / ⛰️ High ground: power spot. Units standing here get +1 to hit.
How to play
1. Click your unit. Blue squares = where it can move. Click one to move (or skip).
2. If an enemy is in range it turns red. Press Shoot or Melee (melee = next to it).
3. Roll d6 per attack die: meet the To-Hit number to hit. Each hit = 1 damage. Wipe out the other team to win.
